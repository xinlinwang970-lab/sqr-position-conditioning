# Code of Conduct for Reproduction Studies

## Principles

1. **Faithful reporting**: All experiment results are reported as-measured,
   without cherry-picking. Negative or unexpected results are included.

2. **Clear attribution**: The original paper's contributions are clearly
   distinguished from our reproduction hypotheses. Design choices not
   specified by the paper are explicitly labeled as hypotheses.

3. **Reproducibility**: All experiments use fixed seeds, deterministic crop
   offsets, and shared evaluation code. Results can be reproduced by
   following the instructions in `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`.

4. **Honest limitations**: Single-seed results, known confounds, and
   cross-architecture comparisons are documented transparently.

5. **Respect for original authors**: This is a reproduction and ablation
   study. The original paper's authors are not affiliated with this effort.
   Discrepancies are attributed to unspecified design details, not author
   error.
